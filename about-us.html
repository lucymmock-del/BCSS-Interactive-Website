Origin

In Estes, group Blue 36 made butter for our special activity. We had mini mason jars and shook heavy cream until we had fresh butter. We had the seasoning options of salt, garlic powder, paprika, and thyme. We ate our butter on off-brand Ritz crackers. This experience was so transformative that we have decided to take the activity back to Mines. :)

Mission

Our goals are to be known for butter in the Mines archives and to maintain the reputation of Freaky Fifth in our own way!! Our plan is to fill the fridge in the 5th floor commons with butter on National Butter Day, November 17th. If possible we will donate all of our butter to a food bank, and we will begin coordinating with local food banks to see if this is in the realm of reality. Whether or not we get the support of a food bank, we will be making butter. :) The food bank will simply determine the quantity. We also want to decorate the floor with butter posters/signs, along with other activities on National Butter Day. The butter experience from Estes will likely be recreated at some point (yay!!!), we will also have other butter themed activities!
